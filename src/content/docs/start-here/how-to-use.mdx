If you've ever used [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) before, you'll find this site familiar.
It is structured similarly to an Obsidian vault, with notes organized into folders and linked together.

## Navigating the Site

You can explore the site in two ways:

- **The Slow Way**: Use the sidebar on the left to browse different sections and pages.
  Click on folder names to expand or collapse them, and click on page titles to view the content.

- **The Fun Way**: Open the [graph view](/start-here/full-graph) to see a visual representation of how the notes are connected.
  You can click on nodes to navigate to specific pages.

## Page Graph View

Each note page has its own mini graph view in the top-right corner (on desktop) or bottom (on mobile).
This shows how the current page is linked to other notes.

You can interact with the mini graph by clicking and dragging to pan around, and using the mouse wheel (or pinch gesture) to zoom in and out.
You may also expand the mini graph to a larger view by clicking the expand icon in the top-right corner of the graph.

## Searching

Use the search bar at the top of the left-hand sidebar to quickly find notes by title or keyword.
Bear with me, though, because search uses a simple text-matching algorithm and is not very sophisticated.
I plan to improve it over time.
